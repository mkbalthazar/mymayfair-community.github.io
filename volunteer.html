Mayfair Committees

Communications Committee Our Communications Committee helps neighbors and leaders talk to each other.
We want to make sure you know what is happening in the community. We always look for clear, easy, and affordable ways to share important news with everyone
Social Events Committee The Community Events Committee hosts inclusive events to build local pride and unity.
By bringing people together, these activities inspire residents to care for their neighborhood, improving its appearance and overall value.
Landscaping Committee Our Landscaping Committee is here to make our shared spaces beautiful and useful.
We focus on protecting our parks, trees, and trails so that Purcellville residents have wonderful outdoor areas to gather and enjoy.
