Welcome to our neighborhood online home!

This site was created to bring you all the community news, events, and resources you need. Stay in the loop and get involved!

Mayfair is a special community known for its blend of old-town charm and neighborly fellowship. We are located only a mile away from the historic Town of Purcellville in Loudoun County. Surrounded by family-owned businesses, antique shops, and farm markets on the weekends. The historic Purcellville Library and Fireman’s Field are just a short walk or bike ride away. These town icons offer summer concerts, parades, and annual festivals that welcome all visitors.

Discover the warmth and beauty of our neighborhood! Surrounded by mature trees and expansive green spaces, our community offers a peaceful, scenic retreat. We invite you to watch this brief video tour to explore our beautiful surroundings, welcoming community areas, and the unique charm that makes Purcellville and our Mayfair neighborhood so special.
