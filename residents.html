Mayfair Neighborhood Residents

Welcome to the Mayfair Resident Resources hub. This page provides quick access to daily neighborhood essentials, including local trash schedules, winter snow removal updates, and community parking rules.

PARKING RULES

Townhouses Parking Rules

  • Liability: Association assumes zero liability for vehicle damage or theft.
  • No Alterations: Residents cannot paint or mark parking spaces.
  • Prohibited Vehicles: No commercial, recreational, oversized vehicles, trailers, boats, campers, or storage pods.
  • Commercial Definition: Rated 1 ton+, 3+ axles, commercial license, or visible business printing/equipment racks.
  • Government Exemption: Police, fire, and government vehicles are exempt from commercial rules.
  • Moving Trucks: U-Hauls are limited to a maximum of 3 days.
  • Inoperable Vehicles: Junk or non-running vehicles are strictly prohibited.
  • No Storage: Trash, debris, and miscellaneous items cannot be stored in parking spaces.
  • Repairs: Limited to emergency maintenance (tires/fluids); oil changes and radiator flushes are banned.
  • Compliance: Vehicles must have current license plates, registration, and inspection stickers.
  • Driveway Exception: Boats/trailers are allowed in personal driveways for a maximum of 10 days if in good repair.
  • Garage Storage: Any prohibited vehicle class may be stored out of sight inside a garage.
  • Guest Limit: Non-resident vehicles are limited to 5 consecutive days.
  • Storage Limit: No vehicle can sit in the same common space for more than 30 consecutive days.

Single-Family Home Area

  • Jurisdiction: All street parking is governed entirely by the Town of Purcellville parking ordinances.
  • Enforcement: Rules and violations are enforced directly by the Town of Purcellville Police Department.
  • Public Streets: Streets are public right-of-ways, meaning the Mayfair HOA has no authority to manage or assign spaces in this area.
  • Resident Responsibility: Residents and guests are fully subject to all local municipal codes, seasonal restrictions, and safety laws.
  • Issue Reporting: Any parking complaints, blocked driveways, or safety violations must be reported directly to local authorities rather than the HOA.

SNOW MAINTENANCE POLICY

  • Plowing Threshold: Equipment typically begins an initial clearing once snow accumulation reaches 2–3 inches.
  • Continuous Clearing: Plows will make multiple passes during and after a storm depending on final accumulation totals.
  • Storage Piles: Snow is plowed aside rather than hauled away, creating large piles that will temporarily reduce available parking spaces.
  • Shoveling Note: Residents should be prepared to shovel out blocked spaces by hand or wait for the snow to naturally melt.
  • Single-Family & Main Road Area: Plowing on single-family streets and Mayfair Crown Drive is handled entirely by the Town of Purcellville.
  • Town Contact: Call Purcellville Public Works at (540) 111-2222.
  • Townhouse Area Responsibility: Plowing and street snow clearing in the townhouse sections fall strictly under the Mayfair Community Association.
  • Common Sidewalks & Mailboxes: The HOA clears common area sidewalks around community mailboxes and common parking spaces within the townhouse area only.
  • HOA Issue Reporting: Any problems, missed areas, or complaints regarding townhouse plowing must be submitted directly to the current property manager.

TRASH, RECYCLING, & PROPERTY MAINTENANCE

  • Resident Snow Duty: Townhouse owners are strictly responsible for clearing snow from their own driveway aprons and the individual sidewalk sections to the left and right of their lot.
  • Curbside Schedule: Curbside collection for household garbage, recycling, yard waste, and bulk items is provided to Mayfair residents every Wednesday by the Town of Purcellville.
  • Morning Deadline: All bins, bags, and bundles must be placed at the curb no later than 7:00 AM on Wednesday morning to avoid being missed by collection crews.
  • Special Pickups: Bulk item collection requests must be submitted online by 5:00 PM on the Monday immediately preceding trash day.
  • Contractor Contact: To report missed bins or coordinate a large bulk removal directly, call American Disposal at 703-111-2222.
  • Town Support Links: General trash questions can be directed to the Town Maintenance Facility at 540-222-3333. Detailed sorting rules are available directly on the Town of Purcellville Trash and Recycling Page.
