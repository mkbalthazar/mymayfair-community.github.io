Mayfair Community Amenities

Welcome to your neighborhood's outdoor amenities! This space is designed to bring residents and their parents, kids, and grandparents together for everyday play, active fun, and relaxation. From weekend family picnics and playground adventures to casual backyard games, this neighborhood hub offers something safe and exciting for every age.

Available Outdoor Facilities

Pavilion

  • Access: Open to residents and accompanied guests only.
  • Prohibited Items: Glass containers, alcohol, tents, bounce houses, and personal electronics (sound systems/TVs).
  • Activities: No smoking, open flames (except the community grill), or wheels (bikes/skateboards) inside the area.
  • Structure: Do not move the furniture, move the trash cans, or make any alterations to the building.
  • Trash: Use the provided bins; if they are completely full, you must pack your trash out.

Children's Playground

  • Access & Hours: Exclusive to residents and their guests; open daily from dawn to dusk.
  • Supervision & Age Limits: Recommended for ages 5–12. Kids under 6 require parental supervision. Use the area at your own risk.
  • Prohibited Items: Pets, glass, alcohol, smoking, vaping, and wheels (e.g., bikes, skateboards) are banned.
  • Conduct & Safety: Profanity is not allowed, and equipment should not be used if wet or frozen.

Multi-Sport Game Court

  • Access & Hours: Exclusive to residents and guests; open daily from dawn to dusk.
  • Supervision & Risk: Unsupervised facility used at your own risk; kids under 6 require an adult.
  • Prohibited Items: Animals, glass, alcohol, smoking, vaping, and wheels (bikes, skates, skateboards) are banned.
  • Conduct & Safety: Profanity is prohibited, and courts must not be used if wet or frozen.
