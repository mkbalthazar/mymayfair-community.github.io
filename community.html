Mayfair and Purcellville Community

Mayfair is a highly desirable neighborhood situated near the historic Town of Purcellville which sits right in the Loudoun Valley, immediately east of the Blue Ridge Mountains. The town perfectly bridges the scenic, rural west and the thriving, modern east of Loudoun County. Residents benefit from an ideal location in Virginia, offering quick drives to the our nation's capital, and panoramic mountain ranges.

Finding your way around the neighborhood is easy! - Use the quick links below to explore our local government, designated Loudoun County Public Schools, local shopping hubs, and favorite attractions right here in Purcellville.

Town of Purcellville

Goverment

Purcellville's local government follows a Council-Manager structure designed to protect our small-town historic charm while ensuring a great quality of life for all residents. The administration handles vital civic operations, local zoning laws, green sustainability programs, and neighborhood security through the Purcellville Police Department. Residents can easily access fiscal transparency reports, town maps, structural projects, public meeting calendars, directly through the official administration portals.

Local Attractions

Visit Loudoun Guide highlights how historic Purcellville seamlessly blends its charming small-town heritage with a vibrant modern lifestyle. Perfect for a summer getaway, the town invites visitors to bike or walk the iconic 45-mile W&OD Trail directly into downtown, where unique boutique shopping awaits. The local culinary scene shines with celebrated farm-to-table dining, legendary local barbecue, and inventive craft breweries, wineries, locally produced rye whiskey, and more.

Loudoun County Public Schools

Mountain View Elementary School

Mountain View Elementary School is located directly behind the community, making it roughly a short walk or less than a mile drive from the back of Mayfair Crown Drive. It is a fully accredited public school serving approximately 660 students from Pre-K through 5th grade and boasts an excellent 13:1 student-to-teacher ratio. Mountain View offers a supportive, high-quality learning environment right in our backyard.

Visit Mountain View School Website ↗(opens in a new window)

Harmony Middle School

Harmony Middle School is a highly rated public school serving approximately 1,140 students in grades 6th through 8th. Strong academic foundation within the Loudoun County Public Schools district. Harmony focuses on providing a smooth transitional environment for young teens through a comprehensive core curriculum, robust elective courses, and a wide array of extracurricular clubs.

Visit Harmony Middle School Website ↗(opens in a new window)

Woodgrove High School

Woodgrove High School is an award-winning, top-performing secondary school serving roughly 1,540 students in grades 9th through 12th. Conveniently situated right next to the elementary school on Alder School Road behind Mayfair community. Woodgrove is widely recognized for its strong academic excellence, competitive athletics, vibrant arts programs, and an extensive selection of over 20 Advanced Placement (AP) courses.
