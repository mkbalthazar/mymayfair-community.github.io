Mayfair Board of Directors

Mayfair Homeowners Association has four members who served staggered three year terms. At the Annual meeting each year, Board positions are elected. The Board of Directors meets monthly. All decisions, financial or otherwise, are made by the Board. This includes items such as Annual Budget, all acceptance of contracting bids, and work recommended by Committees.
